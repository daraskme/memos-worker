## 生成プロンプト

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: one finished square app icon for a Japanese personal notes app named Memos, used as a favicon and a 34px header logo.
Primary request: a simple, stylish, warm editorial icon, more refined than a generic notepad symbol.
Subject: two overlapping ivory memo cards, the front card with a single elegant folded corner; their geometry subtly suggests an M without literal lettering. Bold, memorable silhouette, very few shapes, strong legibility at 16px.
Style: polished flat graphic design with crisp edges, restrained paper-layer depth, thoughtful negative space. A warm golden-amber rounded-square tile that matches a clean white and amber note-taking interface; a tiny deep charcoal fold accent.
Composition: single centered icon, face-on, square canvas, tile fills 92% of the canvas with even 4% transparent margin, paper symbol fills about 65% of tile.
Background: genuinely transparent outside the rounded-square tile.
Constraints: exactly one icon, no text, no letters, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no extra decorations, no pencils, no sparkles, no 3D gloss, no watermark. Keep the symbol readable and elegant at very small sizes.
